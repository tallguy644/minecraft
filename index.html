FAQ

I paid but didn't get my kit.

Make sure you're online and spelled your username exactly (it's case-sensitive). Deliveries can take a few minutes. Still nothing after 15 minutes? Contact us with your Tebex transaction ID.

My inventory was full.

Overflow items may drop at your feet. Clear space before logging in after a purchase.

Can I buy a kit more than once?

Some kits are one-time, others can be bought again. Each card shows its limit.

Refunds?

Because items are delivered instantly, purchases are generally final. If something went wrong with delivery, we'll make it right. See the store terms on checkout.

Is this server affiliated with Mojang?

No. This is an independent community server.
